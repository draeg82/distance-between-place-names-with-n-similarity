Glossary

Character difference
The number of positions at which two equal-length names hold different characters. Nothing is inserted or deleted; names of different lengths have no character difference at all.
Threshold
The character difference being asked for. A pair matches when its character difference equals the threshold exactly.
Dataset
Which places are included in a search, based on population. "Whole database" means every place in the place database.
Place database
The generated table of places that a search reads. Built from GeoNames dumps by the import command.
Scan
One complete walk over a candidate set at one threshold. Pairs arrive as they are found rather than all at the end.
Sweep
A from-a-place search: one selected place walked against candidates over a range of character differences.
Best-so-far list
The best distances found so far, at most top_k of them, in the order the search ranked them.
Leading find
The pair at the head of the best-so-far list, drawn bold on the map.
Focused pair
A find the reader has picked out of the list — the one whose arc is bold and whose coordinates are printed under the map.

Matching, ranking and distances come from this server: every row on this page arrived on a search stream, and every place in the name chooser arrived from a lookup. Nothing here compares two names, measures a distance, or decides which of two same-named places a reader meant — the map bends arcs between the places it was given, and the kilometres beside them are the API's.
